How FairPanel Works

An open-source, self-hosted hackathon system built for complete integrity from first submission to final publication.

1

Registration & Team Formation

Participants register and join an event. Capable captains create teams up to the event’s capacity and generate secure invitation links for collaborators. Race-condition safe acceptance ensures team limits are strictly respected.

2

Iterative Project Submission & Deadline Lock

Teams craft their submission in four guided steps: basics, details, links and media, and review. Draft saves are persisted server-side with optimistic version numbers to prevent stale overwrite conflicts. When the deadline passes, submissions are atomically locked against edits.

3

Private, Blinded Judging & Rubrics

Organizers configure rubric criteria and assign judges using a balanced round-robin algorithm that excludes conflicted team members. Judges evaluate assignments independently in an isolated 3-column review UI. Peer ballots are completely hidden: judges can never view another judge's scores.

4

Conservative Normalization (overlap_bias_v1)

To address differences in judge severity, FairPanel computes overlapping score differences across shared projects. Adjustments are conservatively shrunken toward zero and applied only when judges share sufficient overlapping projects and connected graph components. Constant or isolated scorers are transparently flagged.

5

Organizer Audit & Frozen Publication

Organizers preview standings, resolve eligibility exclusions with mandatory recorded reasons, and inspect coverage warnings. Publishing creates an immutable, versioned result snapshot. The entire operational lifecycle is recorded in an append-only audit trail.
